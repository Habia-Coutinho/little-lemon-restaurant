About Little Lemon

Little Lemon is a family-owned Mediterranean restaurant in Chicago, founded by Italian brothers Mario and Adrian.

Mario brings traditional family recipes and his experience as a chef in Italy, while Adrian leads the restaurant's marketing and helps expand the menu with Greek and Turkish influences.
